## Selected Approach: Midnight Command Center (Approach 3)

Deep navy command center aesthetic with electric blue and gold accents. Split-screen hero, live-updating revenue ticker, animated AI agent status indicators, and diagonal section separators. Syne + Inter typography. This communicates the power, intelligence, and profit-generating authority of the platform.
